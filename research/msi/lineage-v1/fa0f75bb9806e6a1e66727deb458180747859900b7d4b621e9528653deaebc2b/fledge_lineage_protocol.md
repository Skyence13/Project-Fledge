# Frozen-family one-blank studies

The response diagnostic compares a training-selected finalist with its own frozen founder. Both held-out reports, the selection checkpoint and both fingerprints must agree before enrollment. Raw data and enrollment records stay on the research owner.

## Frozen response-curve diagnostic

Use two independently seeded banks of 32 and 64 paired, disposable lifetimes. Both genomes receive the same 32 graded practices, action draws and held-out bearings. On each practiced endpoint present a first light frame with left and right receptor amplitudes `(1−c)/2` and `(1+c)/2`, then the same dark frame. Fixed signed contrasts are −0.8, −0.4, 0, +0.4 and +0.8. Report the native probability of choosing right after the blank for each contrast. Native probability is the logistic transform of the two final motor activations. Each endpoint is branched before every probe; its practice history and genotype remain intact.

The neutral bias contrast is the finalist minus founder difference at equal inputs. Rightward sensitivity is the within-endpoint mean response at +0.4 and +0.8 minus its equal-input response. Compare those sensitivities between genomes. Report the analogous leftward sensitivity as a secondary measure. Both primary effects use a 3-percentage-point minimum and a positive lower paired 95% bound on each independently seeded bank. Intervals resample 2,000 sets of whole matched lifetimes. Each component is classified independently. A positive neutral effect does not establish equivalence on sensitivity.

Measure the original task separately on eight held-out mirrored bearing pairs per lifetime. Report observed left/right blank, visible and dark-only counts, the last-seen direction decoder, and relative effects. Transfer uses unseen contrast strengths ±0.2 and ±0.6 with independently branched endpoints. Score expected accuracy on left and right probes separately. Fresh and post-practice recall and reversal use independent earlier-lesson seeds. Preserve the previous absolute old-skill floors and report every fixed-delay and one-shot change against the founder. A relative drop over five points fails retention. The visible floor remains 75%; the allowed visible drop is five points.

Validation/confirmation roots are 28112811 and 28112812, with earlier-lesson roots 28112813 and 28112814. The registered training root 28112810 is reserved and unused for this frozen comparison. Replicated neutral shift supports testing a selection penalty for neutral bias. Replicated rightward sensitivity motivates selecting for the weaker sensory direction. When both replicate, test a combined objective. If neither replicates, change the intervention or capability through the owner review. No adaptive selection uses these held-out banks.

## Conditional matched breeding comparison

The owner may select this child after a replicated response component. Both arms start with copies of the same verified finalist. Each has eight population slots, three offspring generations, three matched training lifetimes per candidate, the same random seed schedule and the same mutation operator under the 12-neuron cap. Within each arm select two training parents, retain one elite, and mutate seven offspring for the next generation. No practice weights are inherited. Evaluate every initial and offspring population. Freeze one changed final-generation descendant from each arm before validation.

The baseline ranks the weaker blank side, balanced blank accuracy, then visible accuracy. The guided arm ranks weaker-side accuracy plus half its mechanism score, breaking ties by visibility and then balanced blank accuracy. For neutral bias the score is minus the absolute displacement of neutral right-choice probability from 0.5. For sensitivity the score is the weaker of leftward and rightward centered responses. For both components subtract the neutral penalty from the weaker sensitivity. This is a bounded candidate selection method. Adoption would require replication on independent lineages.

Use training root 28112820, validation/confirmation 28112821/28112822 and earlier lessons 28112823/28112824. Compare the selected guided descendant with its matched baseline-selection finalist, the input finalist, and the original frozen founder. For bilateral improvement require at least three points and positive lower paired bounds on both sides against each selection/parent control, five points against the original founder, 75% visible accuracy, at most five points visible loss, intact earlier skills and three-point bilateral transfer gains against the original founder. All criteria must replicate independently. Keep the separate gains, failures and raw sample sizes available when this combined gate fails. The next research decision uses those results.

Subsequent bounded selection comparisons may register a new training-only linear combination of left and right accuracy, balanced and weaker-side accuracy, visibility, neutral displacement and side-specific or weaker-side sensitivity. The new score and complete owner decision are sealed before any offspring are generated. All compute, mutations, held-out measures and default neuron bounds match the baseline arm. To confirm a previously tested selection recipe on an independent lineage, preregister a new procedural founder seed and reproduce the complete intervention and matched control. This tests portability to an independently initialized founder. Its prior training history differs from an archived experienced finalist. The owner records that distinction when interpreting replication.
