# One-blank stationary localization breeding comparison

Enroll the 16 inherited genomes from committed round 5 of `mixed-transfer-trace-r30`. Freeze the population, its report digest and the 16 individual fingerprints before selection. Each of the four source groups retains its registered mutation regime and four slots. Each generation retains one elite per group and breeds three children by mutation from the best two in that group. The neuron cap is 12. Learn during disposable lifetimes; pass only inherited genome parameters to offspring.

Evaluate the founders and four offspring generations on training sequences. Each genome receives six training lifetimes per generation, with matched practice and mirrored left/right probes. Rank genomes by their weaker blank side, then their total balanced blank accuracy, then visible accuracy. Stable group and slot order breaks remaining ties. Choose the highest-ranked changed descendant from the final cohort; freeze its identity and trace its actual founding genome before opening either held-out bank.

Use training seed 28112751. Validation uses seed 28112752 and 32 independent lifetimes; confirmation uses 28112753 and 64. Earlier-skill banks use seeds 28112754 and 28112755 respectively. Each matched lifetime has eight left and eight right blank probes. Score the selected offspring, its frozen ancestor and a decoder that retains the last visible direction on the same sensory sequences. Report left and right one-blank accuracy separately, visible and dark probes, and paired whole-lifetime intervals.

Require at least five percentage points of improvement and a positive paired 95% lower bound on **each** blank side in both banks. Visible accuracy must reach 75% and stay within five points of the ancestor or improve. Fresh immediate recall, reversal, mixed recall and each fixed-delay recall must reach 90%, each fixed-delay one-shot score must reach 75%, and mixed recall must be within five points of pooled fixed recall or better. Fresh immediate, reversal and mixed recall and post-practice recall and reversal may each lose at most five points against the matched ancestor. A failed scientific gate is reported as measured. Stop after four offspring generations, one training-selected finalist, validation, confirmation and research review. No held-out result can change the finalist or extend the breeding budget.
